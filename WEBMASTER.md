# Webmaster guide

You run the Troop 65 website from **Studio**. You never touch code.

**Open Studio:** go to **t65.org/studio** and log in with the account the troop gave you.

Every change works the same way:

1. Make your change.
2. Click the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.
3. Wait about 10 seconds, then reload the website. Your change is live.

Nothing goes live until you click **Publish**. If you mess something up before publishing, click the **⋯** menu next to Publish and choose **Discard changes**.

---

## 1. Change the words on a page

1. On the left, click the page: **Home page**, **About page**, **What we do page**, **Our space page**, **Join page**, or **Troop page**.
2. Click into any box and type.
3. In big text boxes, leave an **empty line** between paragraphs.
4. Click **Publish**.

On the Home page, the fields are split into tabs across the top (Top, Numbers, Tuesday night, and so on). Click a tab to find the part you want.

If you see **[NEEDS INFO]** on the site, it means we don't know that fact yet. Ask an adult leader, then replace the whole `[NEEDS INFO] ...` sentence with the real answer.

Meeting days, time, and address are in **Site settings**. Change them there and they update everywhere.

**The purple letter board** at the top of the Home page is under **Home page > Top > Letter board**. Each box is one line on the board. Keep lines short (about 22 letters). Start a line with `*` to make it gold, or with `-` to make it smaller. Leave it empty and the site shows the standard welcome board.

## 2. Add, remove, or reorder officers

1. Click **Officers (Leadership page)**.
2. Scroll to **Troop officers**.
   - **Add:** click **+ Add item** at the bottom. Type the **Name** and **Position**. Add a **Photo** (see step 3 below). Click outside the box to close it.
   - **Remove:** click the **⋯** on the right of the officer, then **Remove**.
   - **Reorder:** grab the **⋮⋮** handle on the left of an officer and drag it up or down. The website shows them in this order.
3. Click **Publish**.

No photo yet? Leave it empty. The site shows a purple "65" square until you add one.

## 3. Upload or swap photos

Photos are on every page: the big top photos, trip photos, room photos, officer photos, and the photo sets under **Sections** on the About and What we do pages.

**To swap a photo:**

1. Hover over the photo in Studio and click **Replace** (or the **⋯** menu, then **Upload**).
2. Pick the new photo from your computer.
3. Fill in **Describe the photo**. Say what's in it, like "Scouts carrying a canoe down to the lake." Studio won't let you publish without it. This is how blind visitors know what the picture shows.
4. Click **Publish**.

**To add photos to a section** (for example a new campout on the What we do page):

1. Open **What we do page** and scroll to **Sections**.
2. Click a section to open it, or click **+ Add item** to make a new one with a **Heading** and **Text**.
3. Under **Photos**, click **+ Add item** and upload. You can drag in several at once.
4. Drag photos to change the order. The **first photo** shows big. The rest show small underneath.
5. Describe each photo, then click **Publish**.

**Faces cut off?** Click the photo, then click the **crop icon**. Drag the circle onto the most important part (usually faces). The site keeps that spot in view.

Only use real photos of our troop. No pictures from the internet.

## 4. Post announcements and edit links

**Post an announcement:**

1. Click **Announcements**, then the **pencil / +** icon at the top to make a new one.
2. Fill in **Headline** and **Message**. The **Date posted** fills in by itself.
3. Optional: add **Button text** and **Button link** (for example a sign-up form).
4. Click **Publish**. Newest announcements show first on the Troop page.

**Remove an old announcement:** open it, click **⋯** next to Publish, then **Delete**.

**Edit the links and forms:**

1. Click **Troop page: calendar, links, forms**.
2. **Links** are in groups. Open a group to add (**+ Add item**), remove (**⋯ > Remove**), or drag links into a new order. Each link needs **Link text** and a **Web address** that starts with `https://`.
3. **Forms and documents** works the same way. Paste the Google Form, Doc, or Sheet link.
4. Click **Publish**.

## The calendar

The calendar on the Troop page comes straight from Google Calendar. Add or change events in Google Calendar (the troop's `@t65.org` calendars) and the website updates by itself. You don't need Studio for that.

## Stuck?

Nothing you do in Studio can break the website's design. The worst case is wrong text, and you can fix it and publish again. If something looks broken, tell the adult who owns the website accounts (see MAINTAINER.md).
