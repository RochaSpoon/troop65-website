# About

Troop 65 is located in the City of Long Beach, California under the Iron Star District. This troop has been in existence since 1937 and currently has about 40 scouts with 4 patrols. We are located at Lakewood Village Community Church.

Troop Meetings, 1st and 3rd Tuesdays of each month, 7:30 PM

Troop Leader/Parent Meetings, 2nd Tues of each month, 7:30 PM

Patrol Leaders Conference/Board of Review (BOR), 4th Tues of each month
