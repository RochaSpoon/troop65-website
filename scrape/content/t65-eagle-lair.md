# T65 Eagle Lair

Once you reach the prestigious rank of Eagle Scout with Troop 65, you will be documented in our 70+ year tradition of marking the names of all Troop 65 Eagle Scouts in leather, to be displayed in our famous Eagle Lair. Visit Troop 65 in person to see the 200+ names of all of our Eagle Scouts.
