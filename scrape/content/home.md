# Troop 65

Serving God and Country since 1937

  

![](https://sites.google.com/sitesv-images-rt/AMxu72vwH4f8aEBcJkJ7Hr32GZpMaHt_DqnStJFUk1nP6bDLz4DmLka3QQGy10elBJt4dTVj3SGqVYizdZy-zVl-pTCYmEFo8N5ARSlND4aTFpk08RZSfuZCvoF0xDZkM0NkjKTz1Jdj8lrb-84GhDQCSN4QenA3fRY0srH-HWfTCaMoTRT6uMC-msYX3hp5vRNHGE6QalKOsNlDzqdVJr68PLDuR12D_6MUKizzfFicP-0=w1280)

Troop 65 is located in the City of Long Beach, California under the Iron Star District. 

This troop started in 1937 and currently has 40 scouts. 

We meet at Lakewood Village Community Church.

  

Troop Meetings, 1st and 3rd Tuesdays of each month, 7:30 PM

Patrol Meetings, 1st and 3rd Tuesdays of each month, 6:30 PM

Troop Committee Meeting (All Parents Welcome), 1st Tues of each month, 6:30 PM

Patrol Leaders Council (PLC), 4th Tuesday of each month 7:00 PM

Board of Review (BOR), 4th Tuesday of each month 6:30 PM
