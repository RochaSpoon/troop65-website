We may be the newest patrol having only celebrated 15 years in the troop, but we are the ambitious adventurers of 65. We strive to the greatest of our might. We create a fun environment for scouts, so they can enjoy their time at troop 65. Sabertooths is what 65 is all about.

Eagles

The Eagles Patrol is the longest running patrol in troop 65. We are loyal and cheerful when hiking, camping, and going on other events. Our patrol is known for having a high participation in our troop events. The Eagles work hard to foster a community for new scouts to learn leadership and service skills while building the brotherhood that is at the core of Scouting in Troop 65.

The Diamondbacks, built on the Scout Oath and Scout Law, we welcoem everyone to join our amazign patrol. Built on trust, loyalty, and kindness, the Falcon patrol is the best to join. We are supportive and cheerful when it comes to campouts and meetings. Our patrol is a hard-working team that helps others. The Falcons welcome new scouts on all of our adventures.
